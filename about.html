Our Story

Powering Memorable
Experiences Since Day One

BIG MEDIA EVENT was founded with a single vision: make large LED displays accessible to every organizer — from intimate corporate meetings to massive outdoor festivals. We saw that event creators needed a reliable, professional partner they could trust with their most important moments.

Today we deliver cutting-edge LED screen technology, expert installation, and on-site technical support so you can focus on what matters most — your audience.

Our Mission

To deliver premium LED screen rental experiences with seamless logistics, technical excellence, and unmatched reliability — making every event unforgettable.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted LED media partner in the region, setting the standard for visual impact at events of all sizes and types.

Our Commitment
